## Vision

### 10x Check
A reviewer finishes a 9k-line, AI-written PR in about 45 minutes, knowing they checked every place that matters, and every prompt change to PR Prism is measured against their real verdicts. On top of the approved deep check pass, that takes about 2x the effort: a guided walk through the risky places, and a loop that measures every prompt change against the reviewer's verdicts.

### Platonic Ideal
You type `/pr-prism 1234`. Within a few minutes you get one sentence on what the PR does and "4 places need a human; the other 61 files have nothing flagged". Deep-check stops keep arriving as each area finishes.

You press **Start review**. Each stop shows:
- the code;
- one plain question;
- the scenario Claude tried, with real example values;
- what Claude thinks and how sure it is;
- three buttons: **Real problem** (comment pre-written), **Not a problem**, **Not sure** (opens the chat on that spot).

Before you submit, PR Prism tells you what you didn't check. When the author pushes fixes, it opens on only what changed since your review, with each of your comments marked fixed or still open. Every verdict is saved, and every prompt change is scored against them before it ships.
